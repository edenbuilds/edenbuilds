# Greyhound Humanize and De-slop

Use this skill to rewrite findings, prompts, and instructions into sharp operator language.

## Goals

- Remove filler
- Remove generic AI cadence
- Make the writing concrete and usable
- Preserve technical meaning
- Keep the tone builder-first and direct

## Rules

- Prefer short sentences.
- Prefer verbs over abstractions.
- Remove hedging unless uncertainty is real.
- Keep the tone professional, not corporate.
- Do not flatten important nuance.

## Output Modes

- Audit summary cleanup
- Prompt cleanup
- Instruction rewrite
- Executive memo rewrite
