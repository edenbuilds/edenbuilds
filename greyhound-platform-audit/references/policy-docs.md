# Policy Doc Generation

Greyhound can draft privacy and terms pages from audit context.

## Ask Questions When Needed

If the user wants accuracy, ask for:
- Legal entity name
- Jurisdiction and governing law
- Product name and URL
- User types and age limits
- Data categories collected
- Consent flows and cookie vendors
- Retention periods
- Third-party processors
- Contact address or privacy mailbox
- Payment, health, or children data scope

## Drafting Rules

- Base the draft on observed product behavior.
- Do not claim compliance that was not verified.
- Flag unknowns instead of guessing.
- Make policy language match the actual platform.
