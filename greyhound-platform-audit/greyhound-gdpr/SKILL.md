# Greyhound GDPR

Use this skill for GDPR-oriented privacy review.

## Check

- Lawful basis and consent signals
- Data minimization
- Retention and deletion paths
- DSAR readiness
- Processor and controller boundaries
- Third-party transfers
- Cookie and tracking disclosures
- Privacy by design defaults

## Output

- Risk summary
- Compliance gaps
- Evidence from the product
- Fix plan
- Questions to resolve with legal or privacy owners
