# Greyhound Compliance Suite

Use this skill for enterprise compliance coverage beyond privacy law alone.

## Check

- GDPR and UK GDPR
- DPDP
- CCPA and CPRA
- ePrivacy and cookie controls
- SOC 2 control alignment
- ISO 27001 security control alignment
- HIPAA risk flags when health data appears
- PCI DSS risk flags when payment data appears
- COPPA risk flags when children appear in scope
- Retention, deletion, and legal hold handling

## Output

- Compliance matrix
- Risk tier by framework
- Missing controls
- Evidence from the platform
- Owner questions
- Fix plan
